# Deferred items from closed issues

Collected 2026-09-16 from the closing comments of every closed issue (#115
through #237, plus a sweep of the earlier issues). Each closing comment ended
with a "Not done" list naming what the fix deliberately left out; those lists
are gathered here so they can be reviewed in one place later. Nothing in this
document is currently tracked as an open issue unless the status column says
so.

How to read the entries:

- **Source** is the closed issue whose fix left the item behind.
- **Item** is what was not done, in the words of the closing comment where
  possible.
- **Why** is the reason given at the time (design call, scope, or a pointer to
  another issue).
- **Status** is `open` unless a later ticket addressed it. "See #n" means the
  item was handed to that issue; its state is given in parentheses.

Open issues at the time of writing: #114 only (the config redesign; a design
project, not a defect). The pre-#115 issues (#2 through #113) are the earlier
v2 rewrite, its review threads, and the PRD parent; their closing comments
carry no product items that are still outstanding, and the parent (#2) records
every child as handled.
